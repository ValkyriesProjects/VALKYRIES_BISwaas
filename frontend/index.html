Hallmarking Guide

Simple explanations for common hallmarking concepts.

Hallmarking is a system used to indicate the purity/fineness of precious-metal articles through prescribed marks and processes.

HUID stands for Hallmark Unique Identification and is presented in this prototype as an identification number associated with a hallmarked jewellery article.

Gold purity describes how much pure gold is present in an alloy. It may be expressed using karat or fineness terminology.

916 is a fineness number commonly associated with 22-karat gold, meaning 916 parts per thousand gold by fineness.
